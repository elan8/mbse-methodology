# Model checks

Use these checks while modeling. They do not prescribe a review procedure.

- Is the system boundary clear, with external participants referenced rather than accidentally composed?
- Does each architecture specialize its actual system specification, and each integration its chosen architecture?
- Are constituent usages contract-typed, and are selected designs connected through redefinition of those usages?
- Do the selected designs meet parent-local obligations, including tighter budgets and interface conditions?
- Are requirements understandable and assessable, with clear subjects and links to relevant verification cases?
- Do connections, flows, bindings and allocations express the intended engineering relationships?
- Do views expose the intended elements, including inherited features where relevant?
- Are satisfaction claims, planned cases and actual evidence distinguishable?

Run language validation and check the relevant relationships. Parsing alone does not prove engineering compatibility. A view is a presentation of the model, not proof that its requirements are met.

When using results, identify the assessed configuration and conditions. Changes can invalidate evidence. Use project-specific evidence, review and approval practices where needed; Elan8 adds no mandatory role scheme or gates.
