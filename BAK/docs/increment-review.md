# Increment review checklist

Apply this manual checklist to the current engineering question. It reviews the connected answer, not completion of each model area. Acceptance of an increment does not by itself establish product fitness or authorize release.

- **Clear question:** scope, boundary, stakeholders, conditions and useful-answer criteria are explicit; source obligations and engineering interpretations are distinguishable.
- **Coherent model:** relevant scenarios, responsibilities, interfaces and canonical requirements connect correctly. Derivation, allocation, satisfaction and verification make distinct claims. Important semantic relationships have been checked, beyond parsing alone.
- **Justified decision:** consequential alternatives, assumptions and uncertainties support the answer. Merged or unused areas have a short explanation where needed.
- **Appropriate evidence:** requirement criteria and stakeholder outcomes are assessed proportionally. Plans, predictions, synthetic fixtures and executed results are distinguished; evidence identifies its baseline and provenance.
- **Explicit gaps:** unsupported claims, unresolved conditions, residual risks and follow-up questions are visible. Relevant checks pass or their limitations are explained.

Record the question, changes, answer, evidence and gaps in the PR. In exploratory work, a useful answer can be that a candidate is infeasible or further evidence is needed. Humans remain accountable for the engineering interpretation and acceptance.
